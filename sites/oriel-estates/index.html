    (02)  Residence of the season

    The Monolith

    AvailablePalm Desert
    01/03
    i.

    Arrival

    One oxblood wall, forty metres long, broken only by a slot of shadow. The colour is mixed from the iron in the surrounding hills, so that at the end of the day the house seems made of the ground it stands on.

    Bedrooms
    5
    Interior
    820 m²
    Plot
    1.2 ha
    ii.

    The garden reveal

    A full-height cut in the plaster opens onto a walled court and a single palo verde. It lights the living hall from the east and frames the only tree you see from inside.

    iii.

    After dark

    At dusk the pale pavilion lifts away from its red base and a ribbon of glass glows beneath it. The lap pool doubles the whole composition, one line of light laid on another.
