    (01)  Who we are

    Oriel began in Palma in 2009 with one client, one house and a simple rule: never represent a home we would not live in ourselves. Seventeen years later the rule is unchanged. We are twenty-two people in four offices, and we still turn down most of what we are offered.

    (02)  Principles

    Four things we will not compromise.

    1. i.

      Permanence over fashion

      We look for homes that will feel right in thirty years: honest materials, generous proportions, light that has been designed rather than added.

    2. ii.

      Discretion as default

      No portals, no open houses, no addresses in print. Most of our buyers hear about a home from us before it exists anywhere online, and many never see it listed at all.

    3. iii.

      Fewer, better

      We hold seven residences at a time, never more. Every one of them gets a partner, a photographer and a writer, and none of them waits in a queue.

    4. iv.

      Architects first

      The people who designed a house understand it best. We bring them into every sale, every viewing and every renovation conversation that follows.
