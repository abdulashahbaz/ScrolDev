Good to know

How does payment work?

50% to start, 50% before launch. Prices are in USD and fixed for the scope you sign off.

When does the timeline start?

Once kickoff is done and your content is in. From there it’s 7–10 days for Launch, 2–3 weeks for Studio and 3–4 weeks for Flagship.

What counts as a revision round?

One consolidated list of changes, per page or phase. Collect your notes, send them together, and we work through them in one pass.

Who owns the site?

You do. The site, the code and the files are yours on final payment.

Can you write the copy?

Launch includes a light edit, Studio a polish and Flagship copywriting support. If you want every word written for you, full copywriting is an add-on from $400.

What isn’t included?

Domain and hosting fees, paid fonts, stock photos or video, and third-party subscriptions.

What happens after launch?

You get a handover video so you can edit the site yourself, then 7, 14 or 30 days of support depending on your package. After that, a care plan covers updates, new sections and monthly checks.

Will you show my site in your portfolio?

We may feature it in our portfolio and award entries, unless you ask us not to.
